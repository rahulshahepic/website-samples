Our Story

Brewed from a cargo van
and a stubborn idea.

"Fancy coffee shouldn't require a secret handshake."

Cargo Coffee started in 2019 as a converted cargo van slinging espresso at the Dane County Farmers' Market. The van had great coffee and terrible heating — so when a vacant storefront opened on Atwood Ave, we traded wheels for walls and never looked back.

In 2023 we opened our second café on Monroe St, and today both shops roast the same way we started: small batches, honest prices, and a strict no-pretension policy. The van? It's retired out back at Atwood — come say hi.
