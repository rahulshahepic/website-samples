Our story

The regulars were here first

We opened in 2008 with one oven, four tables, and a sourdough starter named Doug. The tables multiplied. Doug is still here.

Everything is made in-house because that's the only way we know. The bread, the jam, the pie crust — if it's on your plate, someone in our kitchen made it this morning.

— The Prairie family
